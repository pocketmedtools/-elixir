## Changes by topic

### Endocrine & Metabolic (done)
- T2DM diagnosis & oral drugs: ADA 2026 CGM from diagnosis on insulin/hypoglycaemia-causing drugs; PCV20/21 or PCV15->PPSV23; RSV vaccine (>=75, or 50-74 at risk).
- Insulin initiation: ADA 2026 CGM on any insulin, no C-peptide/antibody prerequisite for pumps/AID; once-weekly insulin icodec (India, July 2026).
- Complication screening, hypoglycaemia, adrenal: no change needed (already current).
- Diabetic emergencies: paediatric DKA fluids corrected to ISPAD 2022 (10-20 mL/kg over 20-30 min; 20 mL/kg fast if shocked).
- Hypothyroidism: ATA 2026 pregnancy - trimester-specific TSH (~0.1-4.0), fixed 2.5/3.0 targets dropped; confirm mild abnormality before treating; anti-TPO alone does not decide treatment; targeted not universal testing.
- Hyperthyroidism: ATA 2026 - PTU when actively trying to conceive and to ~16 weeks, no preferred drug after; TRAb guides stopping.
- Obesity: 2025 Indian redefinition (BMI >23, stage 1/2); 2024 multisociety GLP-1 RA peri-operative guidance.
- Vitamin D & calcium: Endocrine Society 2023 hypercalcaemia of malignancy - denosumab or IV bisphosphonate, add calcitonin if Ca >14.
